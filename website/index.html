Frequently asked questions

Does any part of my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. The audio is decoded and transcribed inside this browser tab. The only network requests are to download the speech model and the code that runs it, once, the first time you use a given model.

Why is the first run slower, or why does it need internet?

The speech model (40–250 MB depending on size) is fetched from a CDN on first use and cached by your browser. After that, it works without internet.

Can I transcribe more than one file?

Yes — drop several files at once, or add more while one is processing. They queue up and run one after another automatically.

Where are my past transcripts stored?

In your browser's local storage (IndexedDB), on this device only. Open the history icon in the header to revisit or delete them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Does it support languages other than English?

Yes — 14 languages are listed, including Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, and more, plus automatic detection. You can also translate any of them into English.
